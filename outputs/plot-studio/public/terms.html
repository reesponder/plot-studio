Time, breaks and submission

New Builder enrolments include one session with 6 hours of active exam time and one optional break of up to 60 minutes. Existing Builder enrolments follow the timing rules agreed at purchase. Workflow Architect includes one session with 10 hours of active exam time and up to three optional breaks of up to 45 minutes each. For a timed enrolment, the timer starts when you select Start exam in the workspace. A break pauses the active timer until you select Resume or the break ends, whichever comes first.

The server keeps the session time across reloads, browsers and devices. Closing a tab, losing your connection or leaving the editor does not pause it. During a break you must stop exam work. Resume the session before continuing. Allow time for a complete upload before the deadline. An upload accepted while the session is active may finish grading after time expires.

Each enrolment includes up to three scored project submissions. For a timed enrolment, upload each project during active working time in the same session. The portal records submitted attempts, pauses, remaining time and results. The timing controls operate without webcam or screen recording. If you need a reasonable adjustment, contact us before starting your exam. If a service interruption affects your exam, tell us promptly and include the enrolment reference, approximate time and what happened so we can review the session.
