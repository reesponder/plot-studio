From enrolment to certificate

  1. Open your workspace

    After confirming your payment, our team emails you a one-time exam link. Select Start exam when you are ready to open the exam tasks and begin your timed session.

  2. Submit the editable project

    Test your work in Studio, then choose Download .plot project. Upload that file to see your score and the graded checks.

  3. Issue your certificate

    After passing, you can issue a certificate PDF and a public record. A verifier can check the recipient, credential, issue date and status with the ID or QR code.

What happens when time ends?
The workspace stops accepting new submissions. You can still see graded results and access a certificate you earned.
